Pick a packer, a router and a value for each of their options. The page packs and routes one input with them, and shows the joint measures of the benchmark.

- The forms come from the strategy spaces of the two engines (`strategy-space/1`). A new option of an engine gets a control after the next build of the site.
- The **config id** names one point of the joint space: the same options give the same id.
- A range with the tag "site range" comes from the site, because the engine declares no range for the option.
- An option that holds a function, a list or an object has no control. The engine uses its default.
